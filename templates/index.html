Beginner Tips

🧘
Focus on Form, Not Speed
Perform every rep with full control. Slow, deliberate movements reduce injury risk and build stronger muscles than rushing through reps.
🦵
Keep Your Back Straight
For squats, deadlifts, and rows — maintain a neutral spine. Rounding the back under load is the #1 cause of beginner injuries.
💧
Stay Hydrated
Drink at least 2–3 litres of water daily. Dehydration leads to muscle cramps, fatigue, and poor performance. Sip between sets.
😴
Rest Days Are Gains Days
Muscles grow during recovery, not during training. Never skip rest days — sleep 7–8 hours every night for optimal progress.
🍽️
Eat Within 1 Hour After Workout
Post-workout nutrition is critical. Have a protein-rich meal (eggs, paneer, dal) within 60 minutes to maximize muscle recovery.
📸
Track Your Progress
Take weekly photos and note your weights. Progress feels slow day-to-day, but comparing week 1 to week 8 will motivate you to keep going.
